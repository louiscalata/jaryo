How it worksWhat you get

Your pipeline,
visual.

Jaryo reads what your existing pipeline already exposes — steps, inputs, runs, logs — and gives it a graphical interface. It shows only what your backend declares, and asks before anything consequential.

Terminal

jaryo connect ./my-pipeline

Placeholder command. Discovery lists what it checked and what it found before showing anything.

No terminal

Pick a folder or an MCP server; Jaryo shows each step it can see, and marks the rest ? [UNKNOWN].
